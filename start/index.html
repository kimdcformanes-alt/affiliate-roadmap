Choose your focus before you chase every platform.

Beginners often lose momentum because they’re trying to learn Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada, products, editing, hooks, analytics, and posting—all at the same time.

01

What to understand

  • Pick one primary platform first.
  • Know who you want to help or sell to.
  • Start with a small product list you can actually create content for.
  • Add another platform only when your first workflow feels manageable.

Do this now

Write one sentence:

  • My first platform is ______.
  • My buyer is ______.
  • I’ll start with 3–5 products.
FOCUS = one platform + one buyer + a small product list

Pick products because they make sense—not just because the commission looks high.

High commission won’t fix weak demand, poor reviews, unclear value, or a product that’s hard to demonstrate.

02

Check these before you film

  • Does it solve a clear buyer problem?
  • Is the price/value easy to understand?
  • Are reviews and seller trust acceptable?
  • Can you show real proof or a useful demo?
  • Can you create more than one content angle?

Do this now

Choose one product and list:

  • 1 buyer problem
  • 3 benefits
  • 3 possible video angles
BETTER PRODUCT = clear reason to buy + content proof

Stop treating one product like it only deserves one video.

The same product can answer different buyer questions. You may not need another product—you may just need another angle.

03

Seven easy angles

  • Problem → solution
  • Real demo
  • Honest review
  • Comparison
  • FAQ / objection
  • Real-life use case
  • Buyer guide / mistakes

Do this now

Take your product from Lesson 2 and write three hooks using three different angles.

CONTENT = buyer question + clear angle + proof/demo + simple CTA

Track the funnel—not just the views.

A video can get views and still not sell. You need to know where people stopped moving.

04

Read your numbers simply

  • Views: Did the hook earn attention?
  • Clicks: Did the product/angle create buying interest?
  • Orders: Did the listing, value, price, and trust close the sale?
  • Commission: Was the content worth repeating?

Do this now

After your next post, record views, clicks, orders, and commission. Test one improvement next.

IMPROVE = find the drop → change one thing → test again
