Before you purchase

Are the Playbooks just registration guides?

No. Registration/basic setup is only one part. Each Playbook also covers product selection, content strategy, conversion, analytics/tracking, growth, compliance, practical worksheets/templates and a 30-day action plan.

Do I need to buy all the resources?

No. Start with the resource that matches your current problem. If you are unsure, download the free Affiliate Starter Roadmap first.

Does the Planner work with Google Sheets?

The Planner is delivered as an Excel file and can also be uploaded/opened in Google Sheets. Minor formatting or chart differences may appear after conversion.

Are results guaranteed?

No. These are educational and organizational resources. Results depend on your products, content, audience, consistency, platform rules and other factors.

Can I resell or redistribute the files?

No. All files are for personal use only. No resale, redistribution, PLR or MRR rights are included.
